# How to Find Your Username and Computer Name

A quick, no-tech-skills-needed guide. Just follow the steps below in order.

---

## Step 1: Open the Start Menu

Click the **Windows icon** on your taskbar (bottom of your screen, usually in the middle or bottom-left).

*(See Picture 1)*

---

## Step 2: Search for Command Prompt

Once the Start Menu opens, type **cmd** into the search box at the top.

You'll see **Command Prompt** appear under "Best match."

*(See Picture 2)*

---

## Step 3: Open Command Prompt

Click **Open** on the right-hand side (or simply click on the Command Prompt result).

A black window will pop up — this is the Command Prompt. Don't worry, it looks technical but you're only going to type two simple words.

*(See Picture 2)*

---

## Step 4: Find Your Computer Name

In the black window, type the following and press **Enter** on your keyboard:

```
hostname
```

The line that appears right below it is your **computer (system) name**.

*(See Picture 3)*

---

## Step 5: Find Your Username

Now type the following and press **Enter**:

```
whoami
```

The line that appears below it shows your **username**, in the format:

```
computername\username
```

The part **after the backslash (\\)** is your username.

*(See Picture 3)*

---

## Quick Recap

| Step | What to do | What it shows |
|------|------------|----------------|
| 1 | Click Start | Opens menu |
| 2 | Type `cmd` | Finds Command Prompt |
| 3 | Click Open | Opens black window |
| 4 | Type `hostname` + Enter | Your computer name |
| 5 | Type `whoami` + Enter | Your username |

That's it — you're done! ✅
